03 / DENTISTRY + CS

Why
both.

People ask which one I'm going to pick. The honest answer is that I love them equally, and I don't think I have to choose — one is the work I want to do with my hands, the other is how I learned to think.

Dentistry

Dentistry is where my love of biology stops being a textbook and starts being a person in a chair. Enamel, occlusion, soft tissue, nerve — you can see it, touch it, and work on it.

And it's help you can actually watch land. Someone walks in with a problem that hurts, and within an hour you have relieved it. That is the part I keep coming back to: fixing something real for a real person, face to face, the same day.

Computer Science

Computer science taught me how to take a problem nobody has solved yet and break it down until it's solvable — efficiently, correctly, and in a way that still works when the conditions change.

More than any single language, it gave me the thing I use everywhere: the ability to learn how to learn. Hand me a system I've never seen and I know how to get fluent in it. That transfers straight to an operatory.
